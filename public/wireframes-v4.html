Feed page anatomy
Progress pill — mini ring + segmented bar + streak hint. Same on all 4 feeds and inside the reader. Tap → challenge bottom sheet.

G7 leaders agree on new AI governance framework after three-day summit
✦ Key Takeaways
G7 nations signed a binding accord establishing minimum safety requirements for all AI models above a compute threshold.
A new International AI Safety Body will conduct annual audits of frontier labs, with power to issue fines up to 3% of global revenue.
China and Russia declined to join, raising questions about enforcement in non-signatory countries.
≡ Full Summary ▾
Leaders from the US, UK, France, Germany, Italy, Japan and Canada wrapped up the three-day Hiroshima AI summit with a sweeping agreement that goes well beyond last year's non-binding Bletchley Declaration. The new accord requires that any model trained on more than 10²⁶ FLOP undergo mandatory pre-deployment safety evaluations by the newly created International AI Safety Body (IASB). Enforcement provisions include third-party audits, mandatory incident reporting within 72 hours, and financial penalties of up to 3% of a company's global annual revenue for violations. The EU welcomed the deal as broadly compatible with the AI Act, while US tech companies expressed cautious support pending review of the technical annexes. Notably absent from the signing ceremony were China and Russia, whose state-backed AI programs fall outside the framework entirely.

OpenAI announces GPT-5 with real-time reasoning and multimodal memory
✦ Key Takeaways
GPT-5 introduces persistent cross-session memory, allowing the model to recall context from previous conversations without explicit prompting.
A new "reasoning mode" lets users toggle deeper chain-of-thought processing, trading latency for accuracy on complex tasks.
Native multimodal input supports simultaneous processing of text, images, PDFs, spreadsheets, and audio in one request.
≡ Full Summary ▾
Sam Altman introduced GPT-5 at OpenAI's first developer conference in two years, calling it "a genuine step change in capability." The flagship upgrade centres on three pillars: persistent memory, native multimodal understanding, and a selectable reasoning depth. Early benchmarks show GPT-5 outperforming GPT-4o on mathematical reasoning by 18 percentage points and matching human expert performance on medical licensing exams for the first time. Pricing starts at $0.01 per 1k input tokens — a 40% reduction from GPT-4o — made possible by efficiency gains in the new MoE architecture. Enterprise customers get the option to keep memory isolated within a single tenant vault. Competitors Anthropic and Google are expected to respond with their own capability announcements within weeks.

Read mode anatomy
Header: ← back · feed + category label · "Story X of N" · mini progress pill (tappable) · ⭐ mark important

Toggle: Read (active) | Listen — segmented pill below header

Category strip: horizontal scroll of all categories in this session. Active one highlighted. Shows read/total per category (e.g. 3/6).

Content: category badge · headline · ✦ Takeaways (always visible) · Full Summary (collapsible) · Sources row

Footer row 1 — Category nav: << (first cat) · < (prev cat) · Name · 2 of 4 · > (next cat) · >> (last cat)
Footer row 2 — Story nav: < (prev story) · Story X of N · > (next story)
